Privacy

Short version: Resourceful does not collect personal information about students. You can use it without an account, and it never asks who you are.

What we do not collect

  • No names, student IDs, emails, or logins.
  • No accounts and no profiles.
  • We do not store the text of the questions you type, and we do not tie any activity to a person.

What we do count (anonymously)

To understand whether Resourceful is helping, we keep simple, anonymous tallies: how many questions are asked, on which days, and which resources get recommended most. These are just numbers. They are not connected to any individual and cannot identify anyone.

The AI advisor

When you ask a question, it is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to find the resources that fit, then the answer comes back to your screen. The question is used only to generate that answer in the moment. We do not save it or attach it to you.

Links out to USD

Resourceful points you to official University of San Diego pages and services. Once you follow a link, you are on USD's own site, which has its own policies.

Feedback

If you tap "helpful" or report a broken link, we receive that feedback so we can improve the site. Please do not include any personal information in a note.
